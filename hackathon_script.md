# Hackathon Script: Lummina AI
**Time Limit:** 3 minutes
**Theme:** "Making Agents"

---

## [0:00 - 0:30] The Hook & Problem
**(Visual: Show a student staring at a pile of PDFs and recordings, looking overwhelmed)**

"We’ve all been there. You have 20 hours of lecture recordings, a stack of PDFs, and an exam tomorrow. Traditional learning tools—even standard chatbots—are **passive**. They wait for you to ask the right question. But what if you don't even know what you don't know?"

**(Visual: Logo reveal - Lummina AI)**

"Introducting **Lummina AI**. We didn't just build a chatbot; we built an **Agentic Learning Ecosystem**. A team of specialized AI agents that work *together* to actively guide you from confusion to mastery."

---

## [0:30 - 1:15] The "Secret Sauce" (The Agents)
**(Visual: Show diagram of the Agent Orchestrator/Code structure)**

"At the heart of Lummina is our **Agent Orchestrator**. This isn't a simple request-response loop. It uses a **ReAct (Reason, Act, Observe)** cycle with a persistent **Memory System**. It doesn't just 'talk'; it has a plan."

"We've built a multi-agent architecture with specialized personas:"
1.  **The Content Analyzer:** Digs through your raw files (PDFs, lectures) to build a structured Knowledge Map.
2.  **The Quiz Architect:** Doesn't just generate random questions—it targets key concepts based on that map.
3.  **The Grader Agent:** Analyzes your answers to find *why* you were wrong.
4.  **The Study Guide Architect:** Automatically generates personalized cheat sheets for your weak points.
5.  **The Viva Examiner:** Conducts live oral interviews to test your confidence.

---

## [1:15 - 2:30] The Demo (End-to-End)
**(Visual: Screen recording of the app)**

"Let's see them in action."

**1. Knowledge Base (The Content Analyzer)**
"First, we upload a complex lecture on Quantum Physics. In the background, the **Content Analyzer Agent** wakes up, parses the text and images, and stores them in its long-term memory."

**2. Quiz Mode (The Architect & Grader)**
"Next, I switch to **Quiz Mode**. The **Quiz Architect** generates a custom exam. I take it... and get question 3 wrong. Immediately, the **Grader Agent** steps in—not just to give me a score, but to analyze the *gap* in my understanding."

**3. Cheat Sheet Generator (The Study Guide Architect)**
"Here’s the magic. I don't have to re-read the whole chapter. The **Study Guide Architect** detects my weak area and *instantly* generates a one-page **Cheat Sheet** specifically for me. It explains the concept I missed, warns me of the 'trap' I fell into, and gives me a concrete example."

**4. Oral Viva Mode (The Examiner)**
"Finally, to prove I'm ready, I enter **Viva Mode**. The **Examiner Agent** uses speech recognition to interview me in real-time. It’s not a text chat; it’s a conversation. It pushes me, asks follow-up questions, and verifies I can explain concepts out loud."

---

## [2:30 - 3:00] Conclusion
**(Visual: Lummina dashboard or student smiling)**

"Lummina isn't about shortcuts; it's about **active, personalized mastery**. By using a team of agents that share memory and context, we've turned a static study session into a dynamic, 24/7 private tutor."

"This is Lummina AI. Thank you."
